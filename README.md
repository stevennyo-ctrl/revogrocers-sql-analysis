# RevoGrocers SQL Analysis

SQL analysis of grocery retail sales performance and customer behavior.  
RevoU Full Stack Data Analytics — Week 4-5 Assignment (July 2025)

## Project Overview

Analyzed RevoGrocers (fictional grocery retail) sales data to answer 
8 business questions using SQL. Focus on identifying top revenue 
drivers, category performance, customer segmentation patterns, and 
repeat purchase behavior.

## Business Questions Addressed

1. Which product category generates the highest revenue after discount?
2. Relationship between revenue and units sold per category
3. Revenue vs number of unique customers per category
4. Average unit price per product category
5. Price level impact on customer count
6. Category contribution to overall revenue
7. Product categories with highest repeat purchase rate
8. Sales performance summary and recommendations

## Key Findings

- **Confections** = top revenue driver (12.9% of total, $57M+ after discount)
- **Meat & Poultry** = strong secondary drivers (~10% each)
- Top 3 categories contribute **>34% of total sales**
- Repeat purchase rate strongly correlates with everyday consumption categories
- Unique customer count fairly consistent across categories — differentiator is **spend per customer**, not customer reach

## Tools

- SQL (PostgreSQL syntax)
- Google Sheets for visualization
